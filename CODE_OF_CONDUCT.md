# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want every project under Synaptech Solutions to be a welcoming and respectful
space for everyone who participates, regardless of background, identity, or
experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate, especially when you disagree.
- Critique code and ideas, not people. Give and receive feedback constructively.
- Assume good intent, and ask for clarification before assuming the worst.
- Be honest about what you know, what you don't, and how your contribution was made.
- Respect maintainers' time and their decisions about the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission.
- Trolling, spamming, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Repeatedly pressuring maintainers to review, accept, or prioritize contributions.
- Misrepresenting the authorship of a contribution, including undisclosed use of
  AI tools where our contribution guidelines require disclosure.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including repositories,
issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel officially associated
with Synaptech Solutions. It also applies when someone is representing the
organization in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to
g.martinez@synaptech-solutions.com. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as
confidentially as possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate in response to a
violation, including but not limited to:

1. A private warning.
2. A temporary restriction from interacting with the project.
3. A permanent ban from the organization's spaces.

Maintainers may also remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.
